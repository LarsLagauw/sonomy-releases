No servers. So how does any of it work?

Fair questions, answered straight. You don't need any of this to use Sonomy. It is here for when you want to know what you are trusting.

Who hears my meetings?

Your own processor. The model that turns speech into text sits on your disk, next to your notes. The recording goes in, a transcript with the voices told apart comes out, and the sound never leaves the machine.

How can search understand a question with no cloud?

A second model, also on your disk. It reads each sentence once and gives it a place among the others by what it means. A question lands in that same space, and the sentences closest to it are your answers. That is why a question in Dutch finds a note in English.

When I share a page, who can read it on the way?

Nobody. Before it leaves, your machine seals the page for the one machine you sent it to, and only that machine can open it. Whatever carries it in between, a network, a cable, somebody else's computer, is handed the middle picture.

How do two computers find each other with no server?

Directly. On the same Wi‑Fi they see each other at once. Across the internet, they reach each other over whatever way exists between them, and hand pages straight across. Nothing in the middle holds a copy.

How Sonomy finds that way is the one part of it that is not written down here.

What if my friend is offline?

Your change waits on your machine and travels the moment you are both on. A page you each wrote in a week apart ends up the same on both sides, without anyone choosing whose version wins.

What if I lose my laptop?

Every page you shared is already on the machines you shared it with. The rest is a folder of plain files, so back it up the way you back up anything else.

Sealed copies of everything, kept for you on your friends' machines, are the next chapter. Then a lost laptop loses nothing at all.

What does Sonomy know about me?

Nothing. There is no Sonomy server, so there is nowhere for anything about you to go. Not a name, not an address, not how often you open it.

What if Sonomy stops tomorrow?

Everything you have keeps working. Your notes are files, so they open in anything. The app never asks a server for permission to run, so it keeps running. Your friends' machines are the network, so sharing keeps working too.

The only thing that would stop is new versions.
