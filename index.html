Type a slash and choose what you need: a heading, a to-do list, a quote, a card, a formula, even a meeting to transcribe. Drag any block to where it belongs. Open two pages side by side when you're working from one into the other.

It feels light because it is. Sonomy is one small app, and it opens in a blink.

Record the call. Read back who said what.

Start a recording inside your note and Sonomy captures your microphone and whatever is playing on your computer, so a video call is caught whole. When it ends, the recording becomes a transcript right there on your machine, with the speakers told apart.

Nothing is uploaded and there is nothing extra to install. If you'd like a short summary as well, that's an option you can turn on.

Find it by what you meant.

Search understands meaning, not just matching words. Look for “the dinner at Marco's” and find the page you called “Saturday plans”. Ask in Dutch and find a note you wrote in English.

All of it happens on your computer. Your notes are never sent anywhere to be indexed.

Work on the same page at the same time. No cloud in between.

Share a page or a whole project with a friend and edit it together, as if you were at one keyboard. Your notes are files. Files are yours.

Every page is a plain text file in a folder you chose. Open that folder and it's all there, readable by any app, today or in twenty years. Back it up the way you already do.

Sonomy never asks you to sign in, and it never keeps a copy of your notes anywhere but on your computer.
