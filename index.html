Every free app has a bill. You just don't see it.

Servers cost money, so somebody pays. When it isn't you at a checkout, it's you some other way.

  • A free accountPaid with your attention, sold by the minute.
  • A free uploadPaid with your data, sold by the profile.
  • A free startPaid with a subscription, once your things are inside.
  • A free timelinePaid with your evenings. It is tuned so you never put it down.

Sonomy has no servers. Everything follows from that.

Your notes live on your computer. When you share, your computers talk to each other directly. There is no machine in the middle, so there is no bill for one.

  1. No servers, so nothing to run.
  2. Nothing to run, so nothing to pay for.
  3. Nothing to pay for, so no reason to charge you.
  4. No reason to charge you, so no reason to sell you.
  5. Which leaves one job. Make it good.

So these are gone.

Not as a promise. There is no business model to protect, because there was never a cost to cover.

  • Subscriptions
  • Ads
  • Selling your data
  • Sign-ups
  • A feed that picks for you
  • Terms that change on you
  • A new owner

Sonomy is free while it is in beta, and free after. There is nothing to upgrade to.

Most networks get worse as they grow. This one gets better.

Every person who joins brings their own computer. That is more ways for your pages to travel and more people to reach, and still nothing to pay. A platform needs another data centre for every million people. Sonomy needs nothing.

You, and the people you know, and the people they know.No server anywhere in the picture.

Notes are the first chapter.

The same network that carries a page can carry a message, a photo, a call. Everything you use a platform for today, without the platform.

Today

  • Notes that are files on your computer.
  • Meetings, recorded and transcribed, with every speaker told apart.
  • Search that understands the question, not just the words.
  • Writing together, live, with no cloud in between.
  • Sharing with exactly the people you pick.

Next

  • Messages between friends, carried the same way pages are.
  • Sealed copies of your notes kept on your friends' machines, so a lost laptop loses nothing. Only you can open them.

Later

  • Photos, posts and calls, with the people you chose and no feed in between.
  • Whatever a platform does that people actually want, minus the platform.

None of this comes with a date. It is the direction, and the network is already built for it.

Why now, and not ten years ago.

Building something like this used to take a company, and a company needs a business model. Today one person can build it, with AI doing much of the typing.

So there is no company behind Sonomy. No investors, no shareholders, and nothing to pay back.

Start with notes. Bring a friend.

Free now, free to keep. It gets better with the second person, so tell one.
